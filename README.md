## philosophy

We believe the best work comes from proximity. Sit close to the problem, stay
close to the people solving it. Interfaces as clarity, not decoration. Craft as
the thing that compounds. We walk alongside you while you build the vision —
everything else is noise.

## tempo

About a month, start to ship.

Most projects run about a month. Not because we rush — because momentum is the
point. One project at a time, both of us on it, from first sketch to
production.

Week one finds the shape: research, scope, a working prototype. Weeks two and
three are the build — design and engineering in the same room, shipping
continuously. Week four is polish, launch, and a handover that actually holds.

No account managers. No handoff theatre. No six-week discovery. You sit with
the people making the thing, and you watch it get better every day.
