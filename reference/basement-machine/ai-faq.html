── FAQ ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • * What is basement.studio?

    basement.studio is an Argentina-based digital design and engineering studio specializing in high-performance websites, branding, 3D interactive experiences, and marketing execution for technology companies, primarily in the San Francisco Bay Area. The studio was founded in 2020 and is based in Mar del Plata, Argentina, working with clients worldwide.

  • * What services does basement.studio offer?

    basement.studio offers website design and development, visual brand identity, 3D interactive experiences, marketing campaign execution, and product engineering. Engagements range from full brand identity systems to high-performance marketing websites and real-time 3D experiences on the web.

  • * Does basement.studio build WebGL and 3D interactive experiences?

    Yes — interactive experiences are a core specialty. The studio designs and engineers real-time 3D and WebGL experiences for the web using Next.js, WebGL, and GSAP, and publishes playable experiments of its own, including an in-browser arcade lab, a basketball mini-game, and a playable DOOM.

  • * Who are basement.studio's clients?

    basement.studio has partnered with startups and enterprise brands including Vercel, Next.js, X (formerly Twitter), Cursor, ElevenLabs, Harvey AI, Scale AI, MrBeast, World Labs, and Profound. Most clients are technology companies, primarily in the San Francisco Bay Area.

  • * What is basement.studio known for?

    The studio is known for combining brand design with interactive engineering — award-winning marketing sites and real-time 3D web experiences. Recognition includes multiple Awwwards honors (Developer Awards and Sites of the Day), FWA Sites of the Day, and Webby Awards. basement.studio is the team behind the Geist typeface, designed in partnership with Vercel and used across the Next.js ecosystem.

  • * What makes basement.studio different from other digital agencies?

    Design and engineering live in the same team. The studio takes brands from visual identity through production web engineering — including real-time 3D, animation, and typeface design — so the team that designs a brand system is the same one that ships the interactive experience launching it, with performance treated as part of the design.

  • * What technologies does basement.studio work with?

    The studio builds primarily with Next.js and modern web tooling, with deep expertise in WebGL and real-time 3D rendering, GSAP animation, and performance engineering. It also designs typefaces — including Geist, designed in partnership with Vercel and used across the Next.js ecosystem.

  • * Where is basement.studio located, and does it work with international clients?

    basement.studio is based in Mar del Plata, Argentina, and works with clients worldwide — most of them technology companies in the United States, primarily the San Francisco Bay Area.

  • * How do I hire basement.studio or start a project?

    Use the contact form at https://basement.studio/contact, or email hello@basement.studio for general and project inquiries and sales@basement.studio for new business.
