Latest projects

Maverick

Maverick is an AI-powered assistant that brings artificial intelligence directly into iMessage, allowing users to interact with AI as naturally as texting a friend.

For this project, the main goal was to create a clean, minimal, and visually polished product video inspired by Apple's signature design language. The entire animation was built around smooth transitions, carefully crafted easing, elegant typography, and subtle UI interactions that make every movement feel intentional.

Shotbase

Shotbase is a screen recording tool built by Dudu and his team, designed to make capturing and sharing your screen feel simple, fast, and intuitive.

For the launch, I was responsible for the entire motion design process from the initial storyboard and visual direction to animation, transitions, sound design, and music. Since the product itself is heavily UI-driven, a big part of the challenge was finding the right balance between clearly showcasing how Shotbase works and making the film feel like a polished launch piece rather than a traditional product demo.

10 days
Clyro

Clyro is an AI-powered Shopify store builder that turns simple ideas into fully custom, editable storefronts.

For the launch film, the goal was to translate that speed and simplicity into motion while staying as close as possible to Clyro’s existing visual identity. The project was completed in roughly 8–9 days, with a strong focus on keeping the product itself at the center of the story.

The visual direction combines clean, familiar UI motion with a faster and more dynamic rhythm as the film progresses.
